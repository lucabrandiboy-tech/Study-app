Voice changer

Off

Talk into your mic and come out as the robot, live. It uses the voice and pitch picked below.

Wear headphones, or switch off Hear it myself, so the mic doesn't hear the robot and squeal.

Talk instead of type

Off

Say something and the robot says it back in its voice.

Press Start listening, then talk.

Your browser turns speech into text with Google (Chrome) or Microsoft (Edge), so this one needs the internet. While the robot talks out loud it stops taking in words, so it can't repeat itself.

Friends hear it

Off

Send the robot into Discord, Roblox, or any game or call, as if it were your mic.

Your friends hear a YouTube video (or any tab) along with you. You keep hearing it from its own tab.

How to set it up (once)
  1. Install the free VB-CABLE from vb-audio.com/Cable: unzip it, right-click VBCABLE_Setup_x64, choose Run as administrator, then restart your PC.
  2. Come back here, press Find my sound devices, and pick CABLE Input above.
  3. In Discord (Settings, Voice & Video) or your game's voice settings, set the microphone to CABLE Output.
  4. Type with G, or turn on the voice changer or talk mode. Friends now hear the robot.
  5. For a YouTube video: press Add a tab's sound, pick the YouTube tab, keep Also share tab audio on, and press Share.

Discord's mic is now the cable, so your own voice only gets there through this app: turn on the voice changer, with Robot effect at 0% for your normal voice. Switch off Hear it myself if hearing yourself is distracting. In Discord, set Noise suppression to None, or it will cut out music and the robot.

If a game has no microphone setting, make CABLE Output the default in Windows (Settings, System, Sound, Input). Then pick your real mic in this app's Microphone list, and for talk mode pick it in Chrome too (the icon left of the address, then Site settings, Microphone).
